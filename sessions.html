Smooth Arrival Guide

How to Prepare Your Pet for Spa Day

A few thoughtful steps at home before heading to the salon makes the entire grooming retreat relaxed, cooperative, and joyful.

Step 01 • Energy Burn

Pre-Visit Potty Walk

Take a brisk 15-minute sniff stroll right before arrival to relieve bladder tension and expend excess hyperactivity.

Prevents Restroom Urges
Step 02 • Digestion

Light Meal Timing

Avoid feeding heavy meals within 2 hours of bathing to avert bloated tummies, motion nausea, or warm bath restlessness.

2-Hour Fasting Recommended
Step 03 • Security

Bring a Scent Blanket

Bring a small home towel or favorite plush toy. Familiar home scents lower stress cortisol during coat check-ins by 40%.

Home Pheromone Comfort
Step 04 • Transparency

Flag Sensitivities

Tell our receptionist about skin allergies, ear hematomas, recent vaccine injection spots, or hip joint stiffness.
