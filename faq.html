Safety & Health Protocols

For dogs: Rabies, DHPP, and Bordetella (kennel cough). For cats: Rabies and FVRCP. Proof can be quickly uploaded to your Pet Owner profile prior to check-in.
We never lock pets in metal cage dryers or stacked holding boxes. Pets rest in spacious glass suites with plush orthopedic bedding, filtered water fountains, and calming music while awaiting pickup.
We use Fear-Free gentle methods, provide frequent rest breaks on memory foam mats, apply lavender aromatherapy, and avoid loud clippers. We match nervous pets with our most patient senior stylists.
We utilize medical autoclave heat sterilization and hospital-grade virucidal solutions between every pet client, ensuring zero risk of cross-contamination or skin infections.
