Pricing Policies & Transparency

Do prices vary by breed or weight?

Base prices reflect small to medium companions (up to 30 lbs). Giant breeds (over 65 lbs) or thick double coats (such as Newfoundlands) have slight adjustments due to extra drying and styling time.

What is your cancellation policy?

We request at least 24 hours advance notice to reschedule without penalty. Cancellations made within 24 hours can be easily adjusted through your client portal.
